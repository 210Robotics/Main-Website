23 public:
24 /**
25 * Odometry based chassis controller. Starts task at the default for odometry when constructed,
26 * which calls `Odometry::step` every `10ms`. The default StateMode is
27 * `StateMode::FRAME_TRANSFORMATION`.
28 *
29 * Moves the robot around in the odom frame. Instead of telling the robot to drive forward or
30 * turn some amount, you instead tell it to drive to a specific point on the field or turn to
31 * a specific angle relative to its starting position.
32 *
33 * @param itimeUtil The TimeUtil.
34 * @param iodometry The Odometry instance to run in a new task.
35 * @param imode The new default StateMode used to interpret target points and query the Odometry
36 * state.
37 * @param imoveThreshold minimum length movement (smaller movements will be skipped)
38 * @param iturnThreshold minimum angle turn (smaller turns will be skipped)
39 */
