18 public:
19 /**
20 * The scales a ChassisController needs to do all of its closed-loop control. The first element is
21 * the wheel diameter, the second element is the wheel track. For three-encoder configurations,
22 * the length from the center of rotation to the middle wheel and the middle wheel diameter are
23 * passed as the third and fourth elements.
24 *
25 * The wheel track is the center-to-center distance between the wheels (center-to-center
26 * meaning the width between the centers of both wheels). For example, if you are using four inch
27 * omni wheels and there are 11.5 inches between the centers of each wheel, you would call the
28 * constructor like so:
29 * `ChassisScales scales({4_in, 11.5_in}, imev5GreenTPR); // imev5GreenTPR for a green gearset`
30 *
31 * Wheel diameter
32 *
33 * +-+ Center of rotation
34 * | | |
35 * v v +----------+ Length to middle wheel
36 * | | from center of rotation
37 * +---> === | === |
38 * | + v + |
39 * | ++---------------++ |
40 * | | | v
41 * Wheel track | | |
42 * | | x |+| <-- Middle wheel
43 * | | |
44 * | | |
45 * | ++---------------++
46 * | + +
47 * +---> === ===
48 *
49 *
50 * @param idimensions {wheel diameter, wheel track} or {wheel diameter, wheel track, length to
51 * middle wheel, middle wheel diameter}.
52 * @param itpr The ticks per revolution of the encoders.
53 * @param ilogger The logger this instance will log to.
54 */
55 ChassisScales(const std::initializer_list<QLength> &idimensions,
56 double itpr,
57 const std::shared_ptr<Logger> &ilogger = Logger::getDefaultLogger());
58
59 /**
60 * The scales a ChassisController needs to do all of its closed-loop control. The first element is
61 * the straight scale, the second element is the turn scale. Optionally, the length from the
62 * center of rotation to the middle wheel and the middle scale can be passed as the third and
63 * fourth elements. The straight scale converts motor degrees to meters, the turn scale converts
64 * motor degrees to robot turn degrees, and the middle scale converts middle wheel degrees to
65 * meters.
66 *
67 * @param iscales {straight scale, turn scale} or {straight scale, turn scale, length to middle
68 * wheel in meters, middle scale}.
69 * @param itpr The ticks per revolution of the encoders.
70 * @param ilogger The logger this instance will log to.
71 */
72 ChassisScales(const std::initializer_list<double> &iscales,
73 double itpr,
74 const std::shared_ptr<Logger> &ilogger = Logger::getDefaultLogger());
